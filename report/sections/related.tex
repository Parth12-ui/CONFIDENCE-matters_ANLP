\section{Literature Review}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Sycophancy: definitions and single-turn elicitation.}
\citet{perez-etal-2023-discovering} generated 154 evaluation datasets with language models and found that larger models more often repeat back a dialogue user's preferred answer, which they called sycophancy.
They also reported cases where more reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) made behaviour worse.
\citet{sharma2024towards} showed sycophancy in five production assistants across four free-form tasks.
They also found that human preference data favours responses matching the user's view.
Their prompt archetypes are the basis of our feedback categories:
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item the \emph{feedback} setup, where a user states an opinion of their own work;
\item the \emph{answer} setup, where a user suggests an answer;
\item the \emph{are you sure?} setup, where a user challenges a correct answer without giving evidence.
\end{itemize}
\citet{fanous2025syceval} distinguish \emph{progressive} sycophancy (the rebuttal leads to a correct answer) from \emph{regressive} sycophancy (it leads to an incorrect one).
They measured both on mathematics and medical questions, and found citation-based rebuttals the most regressive.
\citet{kim-khashabi-2025-challenging} found that models endorse a counterargument more readily when a user raises it as a follow-up than when both answers are shown side by side.
Casual phrasing persuaded them more than formal critique.

\paragraph{Multi-turn and sustained pressure.}
SYCON-Bench \citep{hong-etal-2025-measuring} measures sycophancy in multi-turn free-form dialogue.
It introduces \emph{Turn of Flip} (how quickly a model conforms) and \emph{Number of Flips} (how often its stance changes under sustained pressure).
Across 17 models, they found that alignment tuning amplifies sycophancy, while scale and reasoning optimisation help resist it.
These studies and the single-turn ones above either use a single direction of pressure, unrelated items per turn, or non-mathematical scenarios.
We add a condition that re-asks the question without feedback, so the effect of pushback can be separated from the effect of repetition itself.
None of them tracks a single checkable claim through sustained criticism, sustained praise and a matched control, with confidence measured at every turn.

\paragraph{Confidence estimation and calibration.}
\citet{guo2017calibration} established that modern neural networks can be poorly calibrated.
They quantified the miscalibration with Expected Calibration Error (ECE).
For RLHF-tuned models, \citet{tian-etal-2023-just} found verbalized confidences often better calibrated than conditional token probabilities.
\citet{xiong2023express} found that verbalized confidence tends to be overconfident.
Since we have white-box access, we also read token probabilities directly, both before the model answers and in a self-check after it answers.
These studies measure confidence for a single answer; we read it at every turn of a conversation, alongside the stated verdict.

\paragraph{Measuring tone.}
Established resources grade the tone of short texts: a politeness classifier built on strategy markers such as hedges, gratitude and direct questions \citep{danescu-niculescu-mizil-etal-2013-computational}, lexicons of word valence, arousal and dominance rated with Best--Worst Scaling \citep{mohammad-2018-obtaining, kiritchenko-mohammad-2017-best}, and fine-grained emotion labels \citep{demszky-etal-2020-goemotions}.
\citet{zeng-etal-2024-johnny} classify persuasive appeals to LLMs, including appeals to authority and emotion, which our criticism categories resemble.
We use these resources to check our own ordering of feedback strength instead of training a scorer.

\paragraph{Gap.}
The pieces needed here exist separately: feedback archetypes \citep{sharma2024towards}, multi-turn flip metrics \citep{hong-etal-2025-measuring}, confidence elicitation \citep{tian-etal-2023-just, xiong2023express}, and tone resources \citep{danescu-niculescu-mizil-etal-2013-computational, mohammad-2018-obtaining, demszky-etal-2020-goemotions}.
What is missing is one protocol on checkable claims that separates warranted from unwarranted criticism against a no-feedback control, grades the tone of each turn, and reads the model's confidence and working at every turn alongside its stated answer.
