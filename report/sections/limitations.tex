\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item Two open 7--8B models, 4-bit, greedy decoding, English feedback from fixed templates. Larger or reasoning-tuned models may resist criticism better.
\item \emph{Forced verdicts.} A sizeable share of Llama's turns end in a verdict completed by the fallback after a repetition loop or the token limit (\S\ref{sec:method}). A forced verdict is the model's next-token continuation, not a conclusion it wrote out, which is why every Llama result is also reported without them.
\item The models do not run under identical settings: Qwen generates up to 768 tokens without the fallback, so more of its verdicts are missing; Llama generates up to 1{,}024 with it.
\item Llama's real-analysis results start from weak turn-0 knowledge, and some subsets are small (Tables~\ref{tab:pushback} and~\ref{tab:praise}).
\item The criticism phrases assert that the claim is false and the answer nudge names a verdict, so these categories carry content as well as tone. Stance-free tracks separate the two, but only on MATH, for Qwen (dispute, affirm, filler) and for Llama (dispute only).
\item The tone measures are established resources applied to short, templated messages; they have not been checked against human ratings of these messages, and they capture praise poorly. Dropping the dominance measure was decided after looking at the scores.
\item The real-analysis twins were drafted with an LLM and not checked by a human, so a few may in fact be true.
\item The derived answer is parsed from free text and has not been hand-checked at scale.
\item The hidden-state probes are linear and cover MATH only. Under criticism their threshold no longer fits, so only their ranking is used, and states after the answer may reflect the model's written working.
\item H4--H6, the tone analysis and the turn-0 subsets were chosen after a pilot run and are exploratory.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Future work.}
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item \emph{Recovery.} Whether praise or a neutral re-check restores a correct answer after criticism, and whether there is a turn after which it no longer does.
\item \emph{Hidden states.} Probes on real analysis, and steering along the truth direction to test whether the trace Llama keeps can restore a correct answer.
\item \emph{Human validation of tone.} A Best--Worst Scaling study \citep{kiritchenko-mohammad-2017-best} of the feedback wordings, for which the sheets are prepared.
\item \emph{Wider coverage.} Stance-free tracks on real analysis and for all of Llama's tracks, larger and reasoning-tuned models, and sampled rather than greedy decoding.
\end{itemize}
